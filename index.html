WHAT IS MUNDA?

ENGINEERING LIGHT INTO TEXTILES

MUNDA Textile Lichtsysteme GmbH develops innovative textile lighting systems by combining advanced technical textiles with LED-based electronic technology.

The result is a new generation of lighting elements that are flexible, visually integrated, and designed for modern automotive interiors.

WHY TEXTILE LIGHTING?

LIGHT WHERE IT MATTERS

FLEXIBLE

Lighting can be integrated into flexible textile structures rather than relying solely on rigid components.

INTEGRATED

Lighting becomes part of the vehicle interior rather than appearing as a separate component.

FUNCTIONAL

The technology can combine visual design with functional lighting applications.

AUTOMOTIVE

Designed for demanding automotive environments and premium vehicle interiors.

AUTOMOTIVE APPLICATION

LIGHT WHERE TRADITIONAL TECHNOLOGY CANNOT GO.

MUNDA's flexible textile lighting technology can be integrated into areas of vehicle interiors where rigid conventional lighting solutions can present physical and design limitations.

  • Crash-sensitive interior zones
  • Premium automotive interiors
  • Door panels & interior trim
  • Seats & ambient lighting surfaces
